# Privacy Policy — Journalist Rater

_Last updated: 26 July 2026_

Journalist Rater stores all of your data — journalists, claims, ratings, and settings — **only on your device**. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no server operated by us.

## What leaves your device

When you use player/club autocomplete or the transfer-record check, the app sends only the search text or a player ID to **api-sports.io** (API-Football), our football-data provider, so it can return matching results. Their handling of requests is covered by their own privacy policy: https://www.api-football.com/privacy

Nothing else is transmitted. Your ratings and claims never leave your device unless you explicitly use **Export data**, which creates a file that you control.

## Data deletion

Deleting the app deletes all of its data. You can also delete individual journalists or claims inside the app at any time.
